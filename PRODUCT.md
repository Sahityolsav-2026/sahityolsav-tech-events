# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hackathon teams working quickly from phones and laptops, plus event administrators coordinating deadlines, submissions, repository archiving, and initial technical reviews from laptops. Users need to understand the current phase and complete the next required action without learning a complex interface.

## Product Purpose

Sahityolsav Tech Events is the operational portal for a time-bound hackathon. It gives teams one reliable place to register, declare an idea, track event time, and submit their final project. It gives administrators a compact control surface for deadlines, team readiness, repository records, exports, and advisory AI reviews. Success means every user can identify what matters now and finish it without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Focused, energetic, and credible. The portal should feel composed under deadline pressure, unmistakably event-oriented, and trustworthy enough for official submissions without becoming bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Do not recreate the previous clobbered collection of identical white cards. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards filled with decorative charts, excessive metric tiles, glass effects, gradients, or empty visual flourish. Avoid dense enterprise software that makes simple actions feel administrative.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the next action and its deadline.
2. Make event progress visible without turning the interface into a chart.
3. Separate operational controls from supporting detail.
4. Use familiar dashboard and form conventions so the interface disappears into the task.
5. Keep the system small enough to remain reliable during the live event.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard expectations. Never communicate status through color alone. Preserve visible focus states, clear labels, readable errors, adequate touch targets, and structural responsiveness from small phones through laptop screens. Motion is limited to the required countdown and immediate state feedback.
